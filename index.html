The island

Our own corner of the atoll.

Lumaré sits alone on a ring of white sand, surrounded by a house reef that begins at the end of your jetty. There is room to be quiet here. Each villa is private, each meal unhurried, and every member of the team knows your name by the first evening.

Plan your stay

Good to know.

Everything you need to arrive with nothing left to organise.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 30 days before arrival. Within 30 days, the first night is charged.

How do we get to the resort?

Fly into Velana International Airport (MLE) in Malé. Our team meets you at arrival and escorts you to a 35-minute scenic seaplane flight. Seaplanes fly in daylight only, so we recommend arriving before 3 pm.

Is the resort suitable for children?

Yes. Beach and Overwater Villas sleep a third guest, and our Sunset Pool Villa sleeps four. A children’s menu and in-villa babysitting are available.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Always. Please tell us in advance and our chefs will plan your meals around it.

What is included in the rate?

Daily breakfast, airport lounge, Wi-Fi and non-motorised water sports. Seaplane transfers, dining beyond breakfast and spa treatments are charged separately unless your offer says otherwise.
